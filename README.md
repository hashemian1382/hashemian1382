### 👨‍💻 About Me
I am a senior **Computer Engineering student at Sharif University of Technology**. My expertise lies at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Software Engineering**, and building scalable, real-world systems. Whether it's training Machine Learning models for medical research, building full-stack RAG AI chatbots, or explaining complex Game Theory concepts, I love turning logic into impactful products.

- 🔭 **Currently focused on:** Advanced AI Systems, Applied Machine Learning, and Modern Web Architectures.
- 💡 **Fun Fact:** At the end of the day, everything is just a well-optimized matrix multiplication... or a handful of zeros and ones!

### 🚀 Featured Projects & Research

Here are some of my most notable works, ranging from AI/ML research to Full-Stack engineering:

#### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
* **[AI Chatbot with RAG & Web Search](https://github.com/hashemian1382/ai-chatbot-public):** A modern, full-stack AI application powered by Google Gemini and Tavily Search API. Features smart query generation, real-time web search (RAG), citation systems (like Perplexity), and conversation management using SQLite/PostgreSQL.
* **[Breast Cancer miRNA Analysis](https://github.com/hashemian1382/Breast-Cancer-miRNA-Analysis-2026):** Replication, verification, and innovation of medical research on serum miRNA for breast cancer detection using Machine Learning algorithms (LDA vs. XGBoost).

#### 💻 Software Engineering & Web Development
* **[University Lost & Found Platform](https://github.com/hashemian1382/University-Lost-Found):** A comprehensive, location-based interactive map application designed to streamline the reporting and recovery of lost items on the university campus, replacing chaotic messaging groups.
* **[tRPC Research & Implementation](https://github.com/hashemian1382/tRPC):** Deep dive and introductory training on modern web development tools, specifically focusing on the tRPC Library for end-to-end typesafe APIs.
* **[Modern Web Apps (React.js)](https://github.com/hashemian1382/HW3_WebProgramming2025):** Various responsive and dynamic frontend applications built during Web Programming courses.

#### 🧠 Interdisciplinary & Academic
* **[Game Theory Narratives](https://github.com/hashemian1382/GameTheory-Narratives):** *(New!)* A collection of narrative-driven essays (available in English & Persian) designed to make complex Game Theory concepts (Nash Equilibrium, Folk Theorem, etc.) intuitive and tangible through thrilling real-world scenarios.
* **[AA Game (JavaFX)](https://github.com/hashemian1382/AA_Game):** A nostalgic mention of my first serious graphical game project built purely with Java and FXML.
