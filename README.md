> Computation is my magnifying glass for understanding complex systems.

- 🧬 **Bioinformatics & Biological Intelligence** — genomic sequence analysis and protein-structure modeling; biology is the largest data-processing system in history, and its synergy with AI is shaping the future of medicine.
- 📊 **Computational Economics & Complexity** — resource-distribution structures, forecasting models, and capital-flow optimization.
- 🧩 **Philosophy of Mind & Cognition** — perception, meaning representation, and the nature of reasoning across biological and artificial minds.
