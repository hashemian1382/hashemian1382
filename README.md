> Computation is my magnifying glass for understanding complex systems.

- 🎯 **Game Theory & Multi-Agent Systems** — strategic behavior analysis, mechanism design, and autonomous-agent interaction. I treat LLMs as strategic decision-makers, not just chatbots.
